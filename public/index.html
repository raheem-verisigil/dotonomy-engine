[ FORMAL SPECIFICATION ]

What is an Outcome Contract?

An Outcome Contract is a declarative JSON/TypeScript specification that sets strict acceptance conditions, provenance requirements, data freshness horizons, and effect boundaries before an action begins.

schema

1. Output Schema

Rigid JSON Schema definitions. If an emitted brief deviates by a single field or key, preflight validation aborts.

fact_check

2. Citation Rules

Every factual sentence must cite a source record ID. Uncited facts fail validation.

history_toggle_off

3. Freshness Horizon

Records older than their freshness limit trigger a CONDITIONAL status instead of a silent pass.

shield

4. Effect Bounds

Strict zero-write enforcement. Effects are declared per Dot and checked before running. Anything above draft level is rejected.

The Two-Tier State Machine Decoupling

The fatal flaw in modern automation is treating technical execution as factual truth. Dotonomy permanently decouples whether a process ran successfully from whether its evidence satisfies acceptance standards.

dns

Tier 1: Operational Run State

INFRASTRUCTURE LAYER

Tracks the computational execution of data connectors, parsers, and prompt compilers.

COMPLETED All API fetches and extractors completed without crashing.
PARTIAL One non-critical data connector encountered network throttle.
BLOCKED Preflight check failed or read-only boundary tripped.

[ TRANSPARENT GOVERNANCE BOUNDS ]

What an Outcome Receipt Proves — And What It Doesn't

Signed receipts show what the system recorded, which checks passed, and that the record has not been altered. They do not replace human judgment.

check_circle

What the Receipt Proves

  • verified
    Input Contract & Version Hash: The exact JSON schema, dot capability versions, and execution plan hash applied to this task.
  • verified
    7 Preflight Policy Gates:A signed record of which preflight checks ran and whether they passed.
  • verified
    Sentence-Level Citation Mapping:Every factual claim links to a source record ID.
  • verified
    Ed25519 Asymmetric Signature Integrity: Proof that the receipt was signed with Dotonomy’s private key and has not been modified by a single byte since issue.
info

What the Receipt Does NOT Prove

  • block
    External Ground Truth ("Garbage In, Garbage Verified"): If a user entered an incorrect number into Salesforce last month, the receipt verifies that the brief faithfully cited the CRM record, not that the record reflects physical reality.
  • block
    Future Commercial Deal Outcomes: A verified meeting readiness brief prepares executives with exact records; it cannot guarantee deal closure or substitute for sales acumen.
  • block
    Substitute for Formal Legal Counsel: Receipts confirm data provenance and policy adherence under contract specifications; they do not constitute legal advice or statutory indemnity.

Questions

Does it connect to my Salesforce or Google account?

Not yet. Today it runs on built-in sample data so you can test the checks and the receipts. A read-only calendar connector is the next step.

What does a receipt prove?

That the system recorded these steps and checks and that nothing was altered after signing. It does not prove the source records are true.

How is this different from a normal AI agent?

A plain agent decides for itself that it is done. Here the plan is checked first, and a separate verifier decides the outcome. Whether that beats a strong agent in practice is what our benchmark is meant to show.

Is it patented?

No. We describe it as a proposed architecture and make no patent or novelty claim.

Will I be charged?

Not today. Billing is not live. The plan is to charge only for runs that meet the agreed acceptance criteria, and we will publish the terms first.
