Privacy

Draft for an early build. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer.

We store your email, a scrypt hash of your password, the receipts you create (IDs, hashes and decisions, not the brief text), and simple usage events such as sign-up, runs and unlock, tied to your account to measure how the product is used. If you connect a calendar, we store its secret link encrypted and read event titles, times and attendee names for the next 14 days when you run a brief. Disconnecting deletes the link. Demo runs on the home page are not stored. We use one session cookie and no advertising or analytics trackers. Apart from an optional calendar link, the trial uses built-in sample data, not your company systems. A formal data-protection review (including Nigeria’s NDPA 2023) is still pending, and we will update this page when it is done.

A contact address for deletion requests will be published here before any real customer data is accepted.
